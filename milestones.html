Project milestones

Project Proposal Progress Presentation 1 Progress Presentation 2 Draft Thesis Website Submission Final Presentation & Viva Final Thesis Publication Evidence

The following dates are based on the official 2026 Regular Batch Research Project timeline.

01

Proposal Submission

Submission of the research proposal defining the overall problem, literature gap, research objectives, methodology and planned contributions of the four research components.

Deadline: 15 March 2026
02

Proposal Presentation

Presentation of the proposed integrated Discus aquarium management system, including water quality, disease detection, breeding lifecycle support, and fish measurement and crowding prediction.

Scheduled: 16–18 March 2026
03

Progress Presentation 1

Demonstration of initial research progress, including dataset preparation, model development, preliminary experiments and implementation work across the four individual research components.

Scheduled: 11–13 May 2026
04

Checklist Submission

Submission of the required research progress checklist and supporting documentation for the first progress assessment stage.

Deadline: 13 May 2026
05

Progress Presentation 2

Presentation of further model development, component-level evaluation, integration of the mobile application and backend services, and progress toward the complete research solution.

Scheduled: 31 August – 2 September 2026
06

Draft Thesis Submission

Submission of draft individual research theses covering the methodology, implementation, evaluation, findings and contributions of each research member.

Deadline: 11 October 2026
07

Website Submission

Submission of the NurtureAqua academic information website containing the project overview, research domain, milestones, documentation, presentation materials and team information.

Deadline: 11 October 2026
08

Final Checklist Submission

Submission of the final research progress checklist and related project documentation ahead of the final presentation stage.

Deadline: 14 October 2026
09

Final Presentation and Viva

Presentation and defence of the integrated NurtureAqua research solution. Each member presents their individual contribution, methodologies, results and research findings.

Scheduled: 19–21 October 2026
10

Website Evaluation and Logbook Submission

Evaluation of the academic research website, including its content, navigation, project documentation and functionality, along with the required research logbook submission.

Scheduled: 19–21 October 2026
11

Research Paper Submission

Submission of the research paper describing the project's motivation, integrated solution, methods, evaluation and principal findings.

Deadline: 23 October 2026
12

Final Thesis Submission

Submission of the completed research theses, documenting the individual research components, experimental methods, validated results, conclusions and future work.

Deadline: 28 October 2026
13

Research Paper Publication Evidence

Submission of the required evidence relating to the publication of the research paper, in accordance with the Research Project module requirements.

Deadline: 1 December 2026
